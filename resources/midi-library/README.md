# GravePain Originals: a MIDI pattern library

2,659 four-bar parts (fills are one bar) in 14 styles, made for GravePain Audio's PATTERNS so the app can ship with
patterns it is free to use in public. Every file is one part, named the way the app's importer reads it.

| Folder | Tempo | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Afro, Ambient, DnB, Funk, Garage, HipHop, House, LoFi, Pop, Rock, Synthwave, Techno, Trance, Trap | the style's range | major, minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Mixolydian |

Each style has 16 **sketches**, numbered 01 to 16. A sketch has one key, one tempo, a verse progression and a chorus
progression, and every part with that number fits the others. Drums come with the first six to eight sketches
of a style (two sketches for each of its grooves); the melodic parts with all sixteen.

    House 03 Verse Drums.mid      House 03 Verse Bass F#m.mid     House 03 Chorus Lead F#m.mid
    House 03 Intro Pad F#m.mid    House 03 Break Bell F#m.mid     House 03 Fill Drums.mid

- **Sections:** Intro, Verse, Chorus, Break, Fill, Outro.
- **Roles:** Drums, Bass, Chords, Pad, Lead, Pluck, Bell.
  - Drums are on channel 10 with General MIDI notes, and the importer splits them into Kick, Snare, Clap, Hat, Ride, Crash, Tom and Perc.
  - The other roles are one part each, with a General MIDI program.
- `manifest.json` lists every file: style, sketch, section, role, key, tempo, bars, progression (as Roman numerals),
  and, for plucks, the arpeggio's order.

## Using it in the app

1. Put the `GravePain Originals` folder in `Private/MIDI`, or name its folder in roots.txt (SETTINGS > YOUR FOLDERS).
2. Press REBUILD under PATTERNS.

The style becomes the pattern's set ("GravePain Originals / House"). Checked with the app's own importer
(`Tools/MidiLibrary/importer-check`):
- the files give 3,789 patterns;
- every file gives at least one;
- no part comes out in a role other than its name says.

## Where it came from

- **Made by** the scripts in `Tools/MidiLibrary`, written for this library in October 2026 by Claude (Anthropic's AI) for David.
- **Not from any MIDI, song or dataset.** The scripts never read one. The bought packs and `Private/Patterns` were not opened.
- **What the rules use:** the styles' tempos, rhythms, progressions and voicings, written out in
  `Tools/MidiLibrary/styles.js`. They are common idioms of each style (four-on-the-floor, a backbeat, i-VI-III-VII, an
  offbeat bass). The melodies, bass lines and arpeggios are generated from those rules, with a fixed seed.
- **No quotes:** nothing is copied from a known song.
- **Rebuilding:** `node build.js` makes the same library again.
